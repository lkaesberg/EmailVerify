# Use cases

The same mechanism — prove you receive mail at an approved address, get a role —
solves a different problem depending on who is on the other side of the door.

<div class="grid cards ev-grid-3" markdown>

-   :material-school: **[Universities & schools](university.md)**

    Let in students and staff with an institutional address. `@*.edu` wildcards
    cover every US university at once, and separate domains can map to separate
    roles.

-   :material-domain: **[Companies & teams](company.md)**

    Keep an internal or partner server to your own domain, so contractors and
    strangers cannot wander in.

-   :material-ticket-confirmation: **[Courses, events & hackathons](communities.md)**

    Gate a server on a fixed allowlist of buyer or attendee addresses, uploaded
    from your ticketing or course platform.

</div>
